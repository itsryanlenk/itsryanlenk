### Ryan Lenk

I find the thing that does not match, prove it, and ship the tool in the open.

I spent 12 years as an intelligence analyst in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard, including a deployment to Kosovo as a counterintelligence analyst. Today I work in life-safety monitoring and run a small research and AI search (GEO/AEO) practice at [ryanlenk.com](https://ryanlenk.com).

What I study: how ranking systems and AI answer engines decide what to surface and what to trust, and how people game them.

#### How I build

- Every claim gets a confidence grade, from proven to unproven.
- Results that went the wrong way get published too.
- Local-first. Nothing sends, posts or publishes without a human click.
- MIT unless a file says otherwise.
